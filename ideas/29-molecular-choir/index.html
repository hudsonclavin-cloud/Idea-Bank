One deliberate simplification. This is a two-mass harmonic spring, not a real molecular spectrum. Peak heights carry no intensity information. Audio divides the physical frequency by 2³⁷; the slow motion above is illustrative.

Read the mathematics

μ = m₁m₂ / (m₁ + m₂). f = √(k / μ) / 2π. Mass inputs are in u; 1 u ≈ 1.66053906892 × 10⁻²⁷ kg. Stiffness is N/m. The isotope comparison adds exactly 1 u to the right input and keeps the same stiffness. The model is an analogy, so the integer masses are illustrative rather than precise isotope masses.
