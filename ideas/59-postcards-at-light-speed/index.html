One space dimension, inertial observers, c = 1. One position unit is a light-second; one time unit is a second. A speed of 0.6 means 0.6c.

The rule under the stamps

x′ = γ(x − vt), t′ = γ(t − vx), γ = 1 / √(1 − v²). Changing the observer changes coordinates, not events. Only spacelike-separated events can reverse their time order; they cannot send one another a signal at or below light speed.
