Quire probe

Two sheets of paper that find out how your printer flips. These four sides are already in booklet order, so there is no page list to type: just print all four. Back to Quire.

Printer that prints both sides

  1. Print this page. Set two-sided on, flip on the short edge (“short-edge binding”). Pages per sheet 1; the page is already landscape. If asked, print all 4 pages at 100% or fit-to-page.
  2. Two sheets come out. Fold each in half, left half behind the right, and slide sheet 2 inside sheet 1.
  3. Read it. You should see 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, every one the right way up.
What you seeWhat it meansFor the real job
1 to 8 in order, all uprightYour driver's “short edge” is the right one.Use exactly these settings.
2, 3, 6, 7 upside downThe back was flipped on the long edge.Choose the other two-sided option (“long edge”, or whatever remains).
Only two sides printedTwo-sided printing was off, or the printer cannot.Use the one-side-at-a-time route below.

Printer that prints one side at a time

  1. Print pages 1, 3 of this document (the two fronts), single-sided, landscape, 1 per sheet.
  2. Take the two sheets out as a stack, do not shuffle them, turn the stack over and put it back in the tray so the printer prints on the blank sides.
  3. Print pages 2, 4 (the two backs). Fold both sheets, nest sheet 2 inside sheet 1, and read.
What you seeWhat it meansFor the real job
1 to 8 in order, all uprightYou turned the stack the right way.Turn it the same way every time.
The backs printed over the frontsThe stack went in printed side towards the print head.Turn the stack over the other way (printed side up instead of down, or the reverse).
2, 3, 6, 7 upside downThe stack went in rotated.Also spin the stack half a turn before re-feeding.
Behind 1 you find 4, not 2 (and behind 3, 2)Your printer stacks output face up, so the sheet printed first was re-fed last.Use the “pass 2, reversed” list or PDF, or reverse the stack by hand.

Two of these can happen at once; fix them one at a time, two sheets each time.
