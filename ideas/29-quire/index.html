Quire

Fold any PDF into a booklet with the printer you already have.

A booklet is a stack of sheets folded in half, so the pages have to be printed two to a side in a strange order: the last beside the first, then the second beside the second-to-last, and so on inward. Quire works that order out, hands it to you as a page list for your print dialog or as a reordered copy of your PDF (made right here, nothing is uploaded), and folds a preview so you can see it come right before you spend any paper.

Print settings

This only works in apps that print the pages in the order you type them. GNOME's document viewer documents exactly this method, and Acrobat Reader and Word are widely reported to honour the order. Browser print dialogs (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) appear to sort the list back into document order, which quietly ruins the booklet; when in doubt, use the reordered PDF, which needs no list at all.

Before you spend paper

Printer drivers disagree about what “flip on the short edge” means, and single-sided printers differ in how a re-fed stack comes out. The probe is a two-sheet, eight-page booklet already in print order. Print it once with the settings above, fold it, and it tells you exactly which setting (or which way to turn the stack) is right for your printer. It takes two sheets and a minute, and after that every booklet you print will be right.

Things that go wrong, and why
  • Pages 2 and 3 come out upside down. The back was flipped around the long edge instead of the short one. Pick the other two-sided option in your driver; some call it “short-edge binding”, some “flip up”, some “tumble”.
  • The page count is not a multiple of four. Every sheet carries four pages, so the document is padded with blanks. The reordered PDF adds them for you; the typed route needs you to add them to the document first, because a print dialog cannot print a page that is not there.
  • The app sorted my page list. Browsers do. Use the reordered PDF, which prints correctly with “all pages”.
  • The middle pages stick out. That is creep: each nested sheet sits a paper thickness further round the fold. Trim the fore-edge after folding, or use signatures of fewer sheets.
  • Half of each page is cut off. The app scaled nothing and the pages were larger than half a sheet. Choose “fit to printable area” or “shrink to fit”, not “actual size”.
  • The last signature is thin. Normal: it holds whatever sheets are left over. Sew it like the others.
