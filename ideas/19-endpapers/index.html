A field guide, three pairs of hands, sixty years.

You play each pair of hands in turn. What the first writes in the book, leaves in it, or takes out of it is what the next one finds. A first reading takes about fifteen minutes; later ones a few. Choices are numbered: tap one, or press its number.

Provenance

One copy of A Field Guide to the Birds of Britain and Europe (Collins, 1954), green cloth, no jacket, pale on the spine.
