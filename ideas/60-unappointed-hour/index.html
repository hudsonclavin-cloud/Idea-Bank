The small rule underneath

Each clock has its own natural pace. It nudges its phase toward its neighbours: dθᵢ/dt = ωᵢ + (K/nᵢ) Σ sin(θⱼ−θᵢ). A small reach can create local agreements without a global chorus. An interruption shifts nearby clocks by half a turn. Changing the controls never secretly resets them.
