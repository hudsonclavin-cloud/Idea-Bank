One encounter. Two descriptions.

AN IDEALIZED PATCHED-CONIC ENCOUNTER. No thrust; a point-mass planet; fixed planetary velocity. The path is a true planet-frame hyperbola, cropped at five periapsis radii. Direction is relative to the planet's motion. Atmospheric drag, moons, solar gravity during the flyby and mission constraints are omitted.
