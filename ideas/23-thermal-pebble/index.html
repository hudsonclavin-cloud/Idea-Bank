The proposal. A sealed, refill-free pebble coupled to a small insulated carrier. Condition below 22°C to reset. The enclosure is a design sketch; the material and hardware have not been built.

The model. Cargo and core share one temperature. Constant heat capacity, fixed melting point and constant heat leakage. No gradients, supercooling or shell mass. This estimates an energy budget, not a certified holding temperature. Read the notes ↗
