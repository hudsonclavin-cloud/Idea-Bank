03 / FROM BED TO WINDOWSILL

Print. Flip. Begin.

  1. Print the broad flat face on the bed, locating tabs upward. No supports are needed for this axis-aligned geometry.
  2. Flip the part onto the jar. The two small tabs go inside its mouth; the 10 mm overhangs rest on the rim.
  3. Rest a suitable leaf node across a rung with the stem through a window. A bare thin stem is not clamped. An optional cotton wick may loop over a rung and trail into the water.

Start with PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers and at least 3 walls. The design is an unprinted prototype: check fit and stability before leaving a cutting on it. It does not seal the jar or control water delivery.

Mesh dimensions are tested. Physical fit, strength and plant performance are not.
