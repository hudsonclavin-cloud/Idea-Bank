What this garden assumes

Euler's equation describes a perfectly straight, slender, linear-elastic column under concentric compression with ideal restraints. It ignores yielding, local wall buckling, residual stress, eccentricity and imperfections. The curved drawing is an exaggerated ideal eigenmode, not a predicted deflection or postbuckling solution. The bamboo-like modulus is illustrative; real bamboo varies and is anisotropic. This is an explorable model, not a structural design check.
