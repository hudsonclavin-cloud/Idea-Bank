Levels

A filled ring means solved; gold means solved in par.

Dividers

A pair of dividers stands on graph paper. Prick every mark.

  • One move: plant one leg, swing the other a quarter turn round it. Tap a dot, or use ← → with Tab to pick the leg.
  • Pins stop a leg landing and stop a swing that passes through their corner.
  • Holes stop a leg landing. A leg may swing over them.
  • Marks are pricked when a leg lands on them.
  • You can never get stuck: every swing can be swung back. Z undoes, R restarts.

Par is the shortest solution the built-in solver can find. Beating it is impossible; matching it is the game.
