How to see it
  1. Print it upright, as it comes, with no supports: it sits on a flat base, and the only downward-facing surface is a 0.5 mm chamfer around the bottom edge. PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers.
  2. Stand a mirror upright on the table and push the pot right up against it.
  3. Look down at it from the angle you designed it for (45°), from as far away as you can. The rim is solved for a parallel view; up close the mirror view arrives at a shallower angle than the direct one (about 3° shallower at 1 m for the default pot, under 1° at 4 m). A phone camera zoomed in from across the room does it best.
  4. Turn the pot on the table until the rim snaps into a clean shape. It does in two positions, half a turn apart, and the two shapes swap between them.
