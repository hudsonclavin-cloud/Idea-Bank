Blinkwheel.

The paper never changes.
Your eye supplies the movement.

THE WHEEL / CUT AND KEEP
Cut the outside, the slits, and the small axle hole.
AN IDEAL GLIMPSE THROUGH THE SLOTS
FRAME01

Print 100%, with fit-to-page off. The sheet includes a 50mm ruler to check before cutting.

Make the object / five small steps
  1. Print the sheet on 160–220gsm cardstock at 100% scale. Measure the 50mm line. If it differs, correct printer scaling.
  2. Cut out the disk. Carefully remove the narrow white rectangles and centre hole, leaving the outer rim intact.
  3. Use a smooth 3mm dowel or skewer through the 4mm disk hole. Cut out the two supplied card washers, slide one on each side, and fix them to the axle with a little clearance around the disk. The disk must turn freely; do not clamp or glue it to the axle.
  4. Hold the printed face toward a mirror and look from the unprinted back through the moving slots. Use bright, diffuse light. Shift your viewing angle until the reflected pictures are visible.
  5. Spin gently. Each slot gives a brief glimpse of another drawing. Change spin direction to reverse the loop. The screen glimpse is idealized; hand speed and viewing position affect the paper toy.

A phenakistoscope, not a video player. The physical prototype has not been printed or assembled.
