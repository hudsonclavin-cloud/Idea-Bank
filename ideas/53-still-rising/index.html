    Still Rising

    Shops, baths, reservoirs, hospital wards, bank accounts, national debts, the air. Each of them fills and empties, and one question about what is piling up answers a surprising amount about all of them.

    This is practice, not a lesson. You answer, you see straight away what really happened, and you go again. Three right in a row clears a rung; seven rungs make the ladder. The first question comes before any teaching, so you can see where you started.

    Twenty-nine questions to climb the ladder if you get every one right; nearer forty-five if you get four in five, sixty if seven in ten. It remembers where you got to on this device.

    The question to carry

    What is piling up here? And right now, is more coming in than going out?
