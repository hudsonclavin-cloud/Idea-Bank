The trick has a clerk. Sorting uses observations and external control. The point particles never collide with each other; every bounce is elastic. This is an information toy, not a free-energy machine.

What is actually conserved?

Each particle keeps its speed. Total kinetic energy stays constant, even when the chambers acquire different average energies. There is no thermalization, measured heat, or simulated memory-erasure cycle. The clerk makes an ideal instantaneous decision for each arrival at the opening.
