What you are hearing

One melody. The lowest voice sings it slowly, from the start. The other voices sing the same melody, note for note and rest for rest, each a fixed number of scale steps higher and faster in a whole-number proportion: at 4:3:2 the middle voice takes three beats for every four of the bass, the top voice two. They come in later, so that every voice ends on the same instant. The fast voices are always behind the bass in the melody and catch it exactly at the last note.

The melody was composed for this seed so that any two notes sounding together make a consonance, except a short note that arrives late and moves by step in and out of the clash; and two voices that move within a fast beat of each other, both straight from a previous note, never land on parallel fifths or octaves. Parallels that are staggered by more than that, or come after a rest, are not ruled out, and most pieces have a few. The strip at the top is the melody itself, with a cursor for each voice; the roll below is every note as it sounds.

Keys: Space pause · ← → seek 10 s · R again · N new seed · M MIDI. Tap the roll to seek. Put #seed=… in the address to share a performance.
