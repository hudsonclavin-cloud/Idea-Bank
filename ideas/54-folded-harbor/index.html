03 / THE FLAT COAST

Everything you need
is already on the page.

Solid lines cut the outer boundary. Dashed lines fold. The striped tabs tuck inside the next wall.

  1. Print on 200–250 gsm cardstock at 100% / actual size. Turn off “fit to page.” Measure the 50 mm bar.
  2. Cut the solid outside line. Lightly score the six base edges and six tab folds.
  3. Fold every wall up 90°. Bend each tab around the corner. Glue its unprinted back to the inside of the next wall.
  4. Work around the harbor, hold each join while it sets, and let it dry before filling.

Paper thickness and printer scaling can change the fit. This is a geometric prototype, not a physically assembled or load-tested product. Keep dry; use for small, light desk objects.

Millimetres in, millimetres out. No installation, accounts, or network calls.
