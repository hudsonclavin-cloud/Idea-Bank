Rimsong

Trace a rim in circles · tap a bowl · rest a finger to hush

How to play

  • Sing: put a finger on a rim and trace circles. It takes a moment to speak, like a real glass. Faster is louder, up to a point; rush it and it squeaks.
  • Ring: tap a bowl, below the rim. Higher up is brighter.
  • Hush: rest a still finger on a rim.
  • Tune: switch to Pour and drag the water up or down. More water, lower note. Let go and it settles onto the nearest semitone. A glass that is ringing slides as you pour.
  • Use several fingers at once for chords.

Keyboard: hold A–K to rub glasses 1–8, tap Z–, to ring them, Space to hush.

Rimsong

A glass harp. You play it the way you play real glasses: a fingertip going round and round the rim.

Sound on. Headphones help. Several fingers work at once.
